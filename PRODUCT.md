# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Pet owners and adopters in Butuan City register pets, learn safe care, browse CAVD-approved listings, find a match, and follow their applications. Veterinarians maintain pet and visit records. CAVD staff review registrations, listings, identities, applications, and transfers. The capstone team demonstrates these workflows to the CAVD office.

## Product Purpose

PawsLink connects pet registration, responsible care, and approved adoption in one responsive site. Its main goal is the adoption bridge: a registered pet becomes an approved listing, an adopter finds a match and applies, and CAVD reviews and records the transfer.

## Positioning

The adoption bridge is tied to CAVD's pet registry and review decisions. Matching supports an application but does not approve one.

## Operating Context

The public can read care guidance and scan a tag. Signed-in owners and adopters use a mobile-friendly portal; veterinarians use clinic lookup and records; CAVD uses review queues and account records. The project is also shown through seeded demo accounts on GitHub Pages.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve all current routes, content, forms, validation, permissions, account actions, Supabase interactions, and demo behavior during UI redesign. The existing site supports registration, QR records, education and quiz, adoption browsing, preference matching, applications, veterinary records, and CAVD review. This visual task covers every page. The site uses React, Vite, and Supabase.

## Brand Commitments

The name PawsLink and its existing deep green `#18251d` and lime `#b7ff72` theme remain. The user supplied a Barkly screenshot as a binding visual reference for bold friendly type, a large pet-photo hero, warm light ground, and playful cards, translated into PawsLink's green palette.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains the current interface, project README, locally stored pet and education photos in `public/`, and seeded demo pet images. The supplied screenshot is a visual reference, not product copy or proof of PawsLink capabilities.

## Product Principles

- Make the adoption bridge easy to find and explain.
- Keep public learning and pet records clear.
- Let each role complete its existing tasks without extra steps.
- Use real pet imagery already available in the project.
